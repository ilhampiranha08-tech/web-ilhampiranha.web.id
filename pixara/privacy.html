Privacy Policy — Pixara Gallery

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pixara Gallery app collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Local Data Processing (100% Offline)

The Pixara Gallery app is designed with your privacy as a top priority. All processing of photos, videos, text, stickers, and image editing is performed exclusively and locally on your device (offline).

We never upload, copy, or transfer your photos, videos, or any personal data to any external servers.

2. Permissions We Request

Pixara Gallery only requests device permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver local gallery and editing capabilities:

  • Photo & Video Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO):
    Used solely to allow the app to display and organize the photo gallery on your device.
  • Camera (Optional):
    Used only if you wish to take a new photo directly from within the app.
  • Biometrics / Fingerprint:
    Used exclusively locally by your device's security system for the "Hidden Folders" feature. We do not collect or store your fingerprint data.

3. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, the security of your photos depends on the physical security of your device.

We provide an additional protection feature (PIN/Biometric) within the app to prevent unauthorized access to hidden folders if someone else uses your device.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If there are significant changes regarding privacy, we will outline them on this page.
