01 // CORE SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

6 Core Pillars of Pixara Gallery

🎨

Pro Multi-Layer Photo Editor & Free Canvas

Professional editing studio directly inside your gallery. Features true multi-layer stacking, dynamic crop with quick numpad aspect ratios, freeform doodling, rich typography, stickers, frames, and precise color grading.

🤖

100% Offline Smart AI Tools (OCR & Object Eraser)

High-performance on-device AI. Extract text from receipts and documents instantly using offline OCR, erase unwanted backgrounds and objects with precision, and transform photos into oil paintings or pencil sketches using native OpenCV Computer Vision.

🔒

Secret Vault & Biometric Hardware Lock

Fortified digital privacy for sensitive photos and videos. Protected with AES hardware encryption, secured behind PIN authentication and device biometric fingerprint scanning. Nothing ever touches the cloud.

🧹

Smart Cleaner & Fast Hash Duplicate Remover

Reclaim gigabytes of device storage. High-speed cryptographic hashing detects exact duplicate images, analyzes bulky video files, and sweeps away residual junk with a single tap to keep internal storage running lean and fast.

🎬

Built-in Video Editor & Batch Image Processing

Essential video and batch toolbox without extra apps. Trim video durations, rotate orientations, compress heavy videos without visual loss, extract MP3 audio tracks, and perform batch resize and format conversions across hundreds of photos simultaneously.

📄

Smart Document Scanner (PDF) & EXIF Privacy Stripper

Digitize physical paperwork, invoices, and receipts with automatic perspective flattening and magic color enhancement, exporting directly to crisp PDF. Strip GPS coordinates and private device EXIF metadata before sharing on social media.
